The Logistics Gap in
Small-Load Cold Chain.

"Mere paas 200kg hai, truck wale bolte 5 ton bhar ke laao. Subah todte hain, shaam tak aadha maal kharab ho jata hai."
Spotted in: Nashik & Jaipur Belt • Source: Local Farmer WhatsApp Groups
Editor's Note

Most startups building for "Bharat" focus on selling software to farmers. The reality is much more brutally operational.

Individual farmers don't produce enough volume to hire dedicated cold-storage trucks. Truck owners refuse to turn on cooling for partial loads because diesel costs eat their margins. As a result, high-value perishable produce rots at the farm gate or is sold at distress prices to local middlemen before noon.

This is a classic aggregation failure. The demand exists. The supply exists. The connective routing layer is missing.

The Builder Opportunity

Stop building farm-management SaaS. Build an operational wedge.

  • Micro-Aggregation: Uber-pool but for cold-chain. Dynamic routing for 50kg–500kg pickups across adjacent villages.
  • Hardware Wedge: Solar-powered, temporary farm-gate cooling boxes that hold produce for 24 hours until the aggregated truck arrives.
  • Market Linkage: Once you control the cold chain, you control the quality. Buyers (restaurants, exporters) will pay a premium for verified un-spoiled produce.
