Terms of Service & Research Protocol

Last updated: May 2026

1. Platform Purpose

GRIPR.STUDIO provides structured problem intelligence based on firsthand operator reports. We help product builders, founders, and research organizations discover verifiable inefficiencies without relying on guesses or synthetic engagement.

2. Responsible Evidence Claims

A problem report published on GRIPR reflects an observed firsthand incident and workaround. It does NOT automatically establish nationwide prevalence, aggregate market size (TAM), or commercial willingness-to-pay. Users of our intelligence must conduct independent validation before deploying capital.

3. Prohibited Usage

You may not scrape, harvest, or reverse-engineer problem case files to deanonymize contributors, spam operators, or generate deceptive synthetic feeds.

4. Commissioned Research Missions

GRIPR Research Missions are delivered as scoped advisory and field interview studies (e.g., ₹49,000 test pilot). Final delivery is subject to custom mutual scoping briefs and research SLAs.
