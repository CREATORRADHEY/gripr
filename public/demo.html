STEP 01 / COMMISSIONED MISSION SCOPE

Commissioned Research Scoping (MSN-2026-081)

Client: RetailFlow Intelligence Labs · Sector: Neighborhood Retail & Vernacular Fintech
SYNTHETIC SCOPE
Hypothesis: Kirana owners lose 8-12% of revenue because they forget to collect customer udhaar, and will eagerly adopt automated WhatsApp reminders to collect dues faster.
Falsification Criteria: Falsified if retailers deliberately avoid automated reminders because debt badgering threatens neighborhood relationship trust and customer retention.
Scoping Deliverable: 5 screened practitioner interviews · Pilot Pricing: ₹49,000 (Experimental Pilot Offer)
