Privacy Policy & B2B Evidence Governance

Last updated: November 2026

1. Platform Architecture & Scope

GRIPR operates as an evidence-first B2B problem intelligence platform. We conduct structured qualitative research missions to investigate operational friction, evaluate hypothesis validity, and deliver Decision Intelligence Briefs for commissioning organizations. We do not operate a public problem feed or commercialize personal contact lists.

2. Information We Collect & Process

A. Client Organization Inquiries: Organization name, business contact person, professional email address, organizational role, and proposed research objectives submitted through mission scoping forms.

B. Qualitative Participant Evidence: Structured interview records, operational workflow descriptions, reported friction points, stated workarounds, and verbatim quotations provided by field participants during research sessions.

3. Three-Tier Participant Consent Framework

Every qualitative research session conducted through GRIPR enforces granular, independent consent tiers:

  • Tier 1 — Study Processing Consent (Mandatory): Informed consent from the participant to conduct the qualitative interview and process findings within the specific commissioned mission scope.
  • Tier 2 — Verbatim Quotation Consent (Opt-In): Explicit permission to include anonymized direct quotations in the client's Decision Intelligence Brief. If declined, findings are synthesized conceptually without verbatim attribution.
  • Tier 3 — Re-Contact Consent (Opt-In): Voluntary permission allowing research operations to re-contact the participant for future clarification or follow-up studies.

4. Privacy Segregation & Tenant Isolation

PII Segregation: Participant names, phone numbers, and direct contact details are stored in isolated collections and are never included in Decision Intelligence Briefs provided to clients.

Tenant Isolation: Research missions and associated evidence are cryptographically scoped to the commissioning organization. Organizations can only access research commissioned under their authenticated tenant identity.

Role-Based Access: Access to participant intake queues, raw session transcripts, and review tools requires authenticated Researcher or Admin credentials verified via Firebase Admin SDK.

5. AI Inference & Veracity Auditing

When Gemini qualitative extraction is utilized, it operates server-side on bounded participant interview text with explicit validation schemas. Models are instructed to enforce strict veracity: financial losses and unsupported assertions are classified as UNKNOWN rather than fabricated. Quotations cited in briefs require exact verbatim match against source transcripts.

6. Contact & Record Inquiries

Organizations and study participants may inquire about data handling or request record updates by contacting privacy@gripr.studio with their designated Mission ID or Participant Token.
