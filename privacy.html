Privacy Policy & Evidence Governance

Last updated: May 2026

1. The Evidence-First Principle

GRIPR operates as an evidence-first problem intelligence platform. We collect firsthand observations of operational friction in the ground economy to help builders solve real-world problems. We never sell raw personal contact databases.

2. Two-Tier Contributor Consent

Every problem report submitted to GRIPR enforces strict consent separation:

Tier 1 — Public Brief Consent: You control whether an anonymized summary of the problem, stripped of names and phone numbers, may appear on our public Problem Explorer.

Tier 2 — Builder Follow-Up Consent: Permitting verified builders to contact you for follow-up interviews is strictly opt-in. Your contact details are never exposed publicly and are only shared after explicit confirmation.

3. Anonymization Safeguards

All inbound submissions undergo manual and automated moderation to redact phone numbers, shop addresses, names, and proprietary identifiers before indexing into Problem Case Files.

4. Research Missions Data Handling

Buyer-funded Research Missions are conducted under formal researcher agreements and mutual non-disclosure agreements (NDAs). Field operators participate with informed consent and receive direct honorariums.

5. Contact & Data Deletion

Contributors may request deletion or modification of their submission at any time by contacting privacy@gripr.studio with their assigned Intake Tracking ID.
