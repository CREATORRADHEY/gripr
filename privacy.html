Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2025

1. Data Collection

We collect your email address when you sign up for early access. We also collect the category you select (Builder/Idea Seeker/Explorer) to personalize your experience.

2. Anonymous Submissions

Problems submitted to GRIPR are anonymous by default. We do not store personal identifiers with problem reports unless explicitly provided.

3. Third-Party Services

We use Google Sheets and Google Apps Script to store and manage waitlist data. Your data is handled securely as per Google's privacy policies.

4. Contact

If you have questions about your data, please reach out to us on WhatsApp via the link on our Thank You page.
