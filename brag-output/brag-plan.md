---

## 1. Rubric
- **What is it?** A B2B first-party qualitative problem intelligence platform that validates commercial market demand and operational friction with real field participants before founders and product teams write code.
- **Who is it for?** Early-stage venture founders, product teams at venture studios, and enterprise innovation directors who cannot afford to waste engineering cycles building features nobody wants.
- **What sets it apart?**
  1. *Real in-person field qualitative sessions*: Not shallow web surveys; 5 screened operational participants in targeted geographies (demonstrated in Jaipur retail).
  2. *Three-Tier Informed Consent Framework*: Explicit separation of Study Processing (Mandatory), Verbatim Quotation (Opt-in), and Re-Contact (Opt-in), with cryptographically segregated PII.
  3. *Audited Google Gemini 3.5 Flash Lite Extraction*: Verbatim quote matching against interview transcripts; unproven financial loss claims are flagged as `UNKNOWN` rather than hallucinated.
  4. *Actionable Decision Intelligence Brief*: Direct Go / No-Go recommendations, operational pain-point rankings, and audited verbatim evidence citations.
- **Strongest claim:** *"Most startups don't fail from bad code. They fail from solving problems that don't exist."*
- **Visual hook:** Deep space navy background with neon cyan glow, cybernetic grid, and kinetic typography.
- **Real UI shown:**
  - Homepage Hero & live pilot metrics (₹49,000, 5 participants, 48h SLA)
  - Mission Scoping Form with Jaipur Kirana parameters
  - 5 Verified Field Operators & Transcript Review Workbench
  - Server-Side Gemini 3.5 Flash Lite Qualitative Analysis Panel
  - Interactive Decision Intelligence Brief with Strategic Recommendations
  - Privacy Policy with Three-Tier Consent and PII Segregation

---

## 2. Angle
Conventional product validation relies on shallow surveys, pitch decks, or biased founder interviews. GRIPR flips this: it operates as an independent qualitative intelligence agency that commissions verified field research, extracts grounded operational evidence via Google Gemini, and delivers audit-grade decision briefs.
