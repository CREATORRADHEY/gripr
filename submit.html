WHAT MAKES A STRONG SIGNAL
Specific beats generic. Reality beats hypothesis.
  • Firsthand: Report something you directly faced, operated, or witnessed.
  • Name the workflow: "Ordering fertilizer during 10-day sowing window" beats "farming is hard".
  • Quantify friction: Time lost, cash locked, crop spoiled, or deals dropped.
  • You don't need a solution: Just explain what breaks and what people do right now.
