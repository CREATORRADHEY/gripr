WHY TEAMS BUILD THE WRONG PRODUCTS

Great teams fail when they start
with guesses instead of reality.

Building software has never been cheaper. Understanding the human and operational reality of complex workflows remains the single highest point of failure.

01 / DOMAIN BLINDNESS

Unfamiliar Operator Workflows

Product leaders entering new industries (agritech, logistics, vernacular retail, healthcare) lack firsthand visibility into the unwritten rules, informal credit, and manual compromises operators live by every day.

02 / SURFACE SOCIAL LISTENING

The Echo Chamber Trap

English-language internet forums and survey panels amplify opinions, not operational reality. High social media likes or survey checkboxes do not establish severe pain or willingness to adopt.

03 / PREMATURE EXECUTION

Engineering Before Evidence

Teams spend months and hundreds of thousands of dollars writing code for an imagined user, only to discover in production that a trivial paper notebook or credit barrier makes adoption impossible.

COMMERCIAL ENGAGEMENT MODEL

What is a GRIPR
Research Mission?

A GRIPR Mission is a fixed-scope research investigation commissioned by an organization to test a specific product or operational hypothesis.

Instead of hiring expensive generalist consultants or wading through generic survey statistics, you receive an evidence brief built on five deep, verified operator interviews.

  • ✓ Three Clear Outcomes: Every brief concludes with an unambiguous recommendation to Reject/Reframe, Investigate More, or Consider Pilot.
  • ✓ Traceable Source Quotes: Every conclusion is linked to verbatim operator transcripts in original vernacular.
  • ✓ Documented Contradictions: We report where operator experiences disagree rather than smoothing over complexity.

RESPONSIBLE RESEARCH PROTOCOL

Evidence over speculation.

How GRIPR maintains research rigor, participant confidentiality, and analytical integrity.

🔒
