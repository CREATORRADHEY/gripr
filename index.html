THE EVIDENCE-FIRST DISCOVERY PIPELINE
"AI made building cheap.
Finding verifiable problems is the real constraint."

Builders scroll social media and guess user pain, while real operators struggle with paper notebooks, opaque pricing, and unorganized logistics. GRIPR bridges the gap through a rigorous four-stage evidence protocol.

01
📥

COLLECT

An operator reports a recent incident, context, and current workaround with explicit 2-tier consent. Vernacular audio, text, or Hinglish.

02

STRUCTURE

Reported facts, affected actors, and friction costs are extracted. Unknowns, sample limits, and unverified assumptions are explicitly flagged.

03
🔗

Possible recurring issues are surfaced as candidate clusters, while distinct root causes are strictly kept separate without premature merging.
